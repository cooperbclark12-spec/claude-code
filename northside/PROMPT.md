# Rewritten brief: Northside Local Facebook growth system

Your original request, sharpened. Use this version from now on when you brief Claude or anyone else.

---

**Goal.** Grow the Northside Local Facebook page into a local audience I can turn into paying clients for my appointment-setting business. The number that counts is **booked sales calls per month from the page**. Followers only matter as the input to that.

**Targets (90 days):**
- 5,000 page followers, almost all inside the Northside service area
- 5+ inbound DMs a week from local business owners
- 4+ booked discovery calls a month that started on the page

**Content: three funnels.** Every post belongs to exactly one:
1. **Reach (T1):** local hooks that get strangers to stop, engage and follow. No selling at all.
2. **Interest (T2):** content that gets local business owners thinking about missed leads, slow follow-up and empty calendars, which is the problem I solve.
3. **Convert (T3):** proof, offers and calls to action that turn a viewer into a booked call.

**Mix:** 60% T1, 30% T2, 10% T3 at launch. Shift toward 50/30/20 after 1,000 followers.

**Production.** I send a one-line idea. Claude:
1. picks the tier
2. writes the hook, script and caption
3. builds a 9:16 motion-graphics video (HyperFrames / Claude motion) in my brand style
4. renders it to MP4
5. **shows it to me for approval**
6. schedules it to the Northside Local page through the Porter Metrics MCP, in the next open slot on the calendar
7. logs it so performance can be pulled back from Page Insights each week

**My schedule:** [FILL IN: hours I work setting and outreach, and the one weekly block I'll spend approving content]. Any engagement work the plan gives me (comment replies, DMs) has to fit around that.

**Constraints:** Nothing posts without my approval. Every T3 post has one clear CTA (DM a keyword or book a call). Every week ends with a report: reach, follows, DMs and calls for each tier.

**Deliverables:** the strategy and calendar (`northside/STRATEGY.md`) and a reusable Claude skill that runs the pipeline (`.claude/skills/northside-post/SKILL.md`).
