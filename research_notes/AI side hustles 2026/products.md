# AI Product Side Hustles 2026: digital products, POD, KDP, micro-SaaS/GPTs, vibe-coded apps

Note: Sources came from web search result summaries (Nov 2026), not full-page fetches. Many earnings figures come from vendors or modelled estimates and are flagged as such.

## 1. Etsy / Amazon KDP policies on AI content, saturation, earnings distributions

### Takeaway
AI content is still allowed on both platforms, but only with disclosure. Etsy tightened its rules sharply in October 2026: it banned AI-generated instructional downloads and bot-made listings. KDP still caps uploads at about 3 titles/day and requires AI disclosure. Saturation is measurable: AI-heavy books now make up about 20% of a genre-fiction sample but earn below their share.

### Cited Findings
- KDP caps new titles at 3 per day per account (September 2023). Amazon said the cap "may be adjusted", that few publishers would be affected, and that exceptions can be requested. — [Authors Guild](https://authorsguild.org/news/amazon-adds-to-kdp-generative-ai-policy-caps-daily-self-publishing-uploads/); [Publishers Weekly](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/digital/content-and-e-books/article/93207-kdp-will-limit-daily-number-of-new-titles.html); [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2023/09/22/amazon_ai_book_publishing_limit/)
- KDP does not ban AI books. Publishers must tell Amazon about AI-generated text, images or translations when publishing or republishing. — [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/amazon-restricts-author-self-publishing-3-books-a-day-1850864471); [Scripps News](https://scrippsnews.com/stories/amazon-limits-self-publishers-to-3-books-per-day-citing-ai-concerns)
- A vendor guide says the cap is "currently" in force and claims title suppression in 2025–26. Amazon is not cited, so this is unverified. — [ScribeCount](https://scribecount.com/author-resource/protecting-books/kdp-ai-content-policy-the-compliance-guide)
- Study of about 14,000 self-published genre-fiction ebooks: books with substantial AI content are about 20% of the catalog but only 12.1% of sales and 11.3% of revenue. Their share of top-25 slots rose from near 0 to 31%. — [alphaXiv paper 2607.20349](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2607.20349); coverage [Fortune, Sep 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/09/14/ai-slop-books-amazon-marketplace-publishing-human-authors/); [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/ai-generated-books-are-flooding-amazon-and-tanking-sales-for-human-authors/)
- Kindle Unlimited dilution: in 2023 the KU catalog grew 18% while page-read royalties grew 12%, so the per-page rate fell about 5.5%. — [Complete AI Training summary](https://completeaitraining.com/news/amazons-self-published-catalog-grows-38-fold-as-human.md) (secondary)
- Saturation is concentrated in business guides, motivational, how-to and trope-driven genre fiction. — [Startup Fortune, Jul 2026](https://startupfortune.com/amazons-e-book-market-is-getting-flooded-by-ai-generated-titles-and-the-discoverability-crisis-is-just-beginning/)
- Etsy AI update, 22 Oct 2026:
  - AI-made digital downloads (planners, journals, ebooks, art) are still allowed if the seller plays a clear creative role and discloses AI use.
  - AI-generated instructional/functional downloads are banned: knitting, crochet and sewing patterns, recipes, assembly instructions, and per some reports woodworking plans.
  - Bot-generated listings with no human involvement are removed and the shop may be removed.
  - Digital downloads must include a visual preview.
  - Detection will expand to existing listings.
  - Sources: [Digital Commerce 360](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/10/26/etsy-ai-policy/amp/); [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/etsy-bans-ai-patterns/); [Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/?p=320422); [Etsy House Rules](https://www.etsy.com/new-guidelines)
- Etsy's earlier 2024 policy allowed AI art when disclosed, under a "created by"/"designed by" framework. — [TechCrunch 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/09/etsy-new-seller-policy-2024-generative-ai)
- Etsy now also requires at least one unaltered photo per listing. — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/etsy-forces-sellers-to-show-one-unaltered-photo-in-every-listing/)

### Inferences
- The "mass-upload AI planners/patterns" Etsy playbook is now a policy risk, not just a saturation problem. Patterns and recipes are outright banned.
- On KDP, volume plays are capped and diluted. Under-indexed revenue suggests most AI books earn little, though the sample is fiction only.

### Gaps
- No primary Amazon page from 2026 confirming the current cap number. No Amazon data on the distribution of KDP earnings.
- No Etsy-published distribution of seller income. I did not read Etsy's full policy text, only secondary coverage.

## 2. Gumroad/Etsy seller earnings; print-on-demand margins

### Takeaway
Earnings are heavily concentrated. The median Gumroad creator makes about $70/month, a figure that has been roughly flat since 2020, and a large share of products earn $0. POD net margins realistically run 20–35% after fees and ads. All POD figures come from vendors.

### Cited Findings
- Gumroad 2026 estimates from InsightRaider's model, which multiplies ratings by price by a conversion multiplier (modelled, not reported):
  - median creator about $72/month
  - top 1% earn $10k+/month
  - 44% of products earn $0
  - median product: 28 sales at a $13 price
  - the top-1% revenue share is inconsistent across its own pages (77.3% vs 99.5%)
  - Sources: [InsightRaider State of Gumroad 2026](https://insightraider.com/en/state-of-gumroad-2026); [dev.to summary](https://dev.to/insightraider/72month-thats-what-the-median-gumroad-creator-earns-these-5-niches-break-the-pattern-49f0)
- Gumroad's own 2020 data: median creator earned about $70, and the top 1% took about 60% of the money. — [Silicon Florist](https://siliconflorist.com/2021/01/12/what-happened-in-the-gumroad-creator-community-in-2020/)
- POD margins:
  - Printful says 20–40% is typical: mugs 10–30%, blankets 30–50%, totes 15–30%. — [Printful](https://www.printful.com/blog/what-is-a-good-profit-margin-for-print-on-demand)
  - Printify says 30–50%. — [Printify](https://printify.com/blog/is-print-on-demand-profitable/)
  - MyDesigns puts realistic net at 20–35% after shipping, Etsy fees, payment processing and an ad buffer. — [MyDesigns](https://mydesigns.io/blog/print-on-demand-profit-margin/)
- Printify claims a median Etsy seller income of about $574/month, with the top 1% at $50k. This is a vendor claim without a published survey. — [Printify](https://printify.com/blog/how-much-can-you-make-on-etsy/)
- An unsourced guide says POD beginners earn $50–500/month. — [Wealthvieu](https://wealthvieu.com/print-on-demand-income-guide)

### Inferences
- Startup cost is low: $0–100 for tools and listing fees, plus an optional ad budget. Revenue per sale is thin, often a few dollars per POD item, so the business needs volume or traffic.
- Time to first dollar is likely weeks to months, and many sellers never get there (44% of Gumroad products earn $0). This is an inference from the distribution data.

### Gaps
- No independent survey of Etsy or POD seller earnings. No AI-specific breakdown of seller earnings.

## 3. Micro-SaaS success rates, verified MRR, GPT Store revenue sharing, vibe-coded apps

### Takeaway
Most indie SaaS stays tiny: 28% are under $1k MRR in MicroConf's survey, and 65% have only 1–10 paying customers. Stripe-verified directories like TrustMRR exist, but they skew toward survivors. GPT Store revenue sharing never became a meaningful income source. ChatGPT Apps SDK monetization is limited to physical goods plus a beta checkout. Vibe-coding platforms are booming (Lovable over $500M ARR), but the platforms disclose no data on builders' income.

### Cited Findings
- MicroConf State of Indie SaaS 2024 (469 founders, data from late 2023): 28% under $1k MRR, the largest band. 65% have 1–10 paying customers, and more than half have fewer than 50. — [MicroConf](https://microconf.com/founders/0-10k-arr)
- Community estimate: about 50% of active indie hackers are at $0–1k MRR, and under 5% ever pass $100k. This is an approximate figure. — [mean.ceo blog](https://blog.mean.ceo/?p=11175)
- Indie Hackers revenue figures are self-reported. TrustMRR verifies revenue through Stripe, LemonSqueezy or Paddle API keys. Its size is reported as 800–1,000+ verified startups and over $1B in total verified revenue; the figures differ by source. — [TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/trustmrr); [MakerStack review](https://makerstack.co/reviews/trustmrr-review/); [ExitBid review](https://exitbid.io/blog/trustmrr-review-2026)
- Verified example of the stated flow: a TrustMRR listing marked "Revenue is verified with Stripe API key". — [TrustMRR Verdikt](https://trustmrr.com/startup/verdikt?metric=mrr)
- Self-reported Indie Hackers story: $125k MRR solo founder. — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/c4o2Tfs6mjdpip5yZhaO)
- Counter-example: "$0 MRR, 3 users" after months of building with no marketing. — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/0-mrr-3-users-months-of-building-heres-what-happens-when-you-build-a-saas-and-forget-to-market-it-5528a64f97)
- GPT Store history:
  - The revenue program was promised for Q1 2024, with US builders to be paid on engagement. — [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-the-gpt-store/?re=true); [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/openai-launches-gpt-store-but-revenue-sharing-is-still-to-come)
  - It was then tested only with a few top US creators, and builders outside the US got no guidance. — [OpenAI forum](https://community.openai.com/t/what-is-the-status-with-gpt-store-revenue-share/839172); [forum](https://community.openai.com/t/guidance-for-gpt-store-builders-outside-the-us/1357911)
- ChatGPT Apps SDK:
  - Developers may link out for physical-goods transactions only. Digital goods are being "explored". — [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/developers-can-now-submit-apps-to-chatgpt/)
  - As of January 2026, digital subscriptions and tiers were not supported. — [OpenAI forum](https://community.openai.com/t/chatgpt-app-monetization-apps-sdk/1372343)
  - Instant Checkout/ACP is in limited private beta. — [Apps SDK monetization docs](https://developers.openai.com/apps-sdk/build/monetization)
- Lovable reported over $500M annualized revenue in June 2026, with 1M new projects a week. Some users have reached five- and six-figure revenue, but Lovable disclosed no distribution. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/09/lovable-says-it-has-hit-500m-in-annualized-revenue-with-1-million-new-projects-a-week/)
- A $600M ARR claim (Aug–Sep 2026) is unconfirmed. — [AI Magazine](https://aimagazine.com/news/lovable-europes-vibe-coding-platform-crosses-us-600m-arr)
- One cited study found 65% of scanned vibe-coded apps had security vulnerabilities, and 58% had at least one critical flaw. — [findskill.ai](https://findskill.ai/blog/vibe-coding-by-the-numbers/) (secondary)

### Inferences
- The money in vibe coding flows mainly to the platforms (picks-and-shovels), not to the median builder.
- Startup cost for micro-SaaS is low: tool subscriptions of about $20–100/month plus API costs. Distribution is the bottleneck.
- Verified-revenue directories show survivors, so they suffer from survivorship bias.
- GPTs and ChatGPT apps are best treated as lead generation for products sold off-platform, not as direct income.

### Gaps
- No official 2026 OpenAI statement on GPT Store payouts. No data on payout amounts.
- No rigorous success-rate data specific to AI wrappers or micro-SaaS. No Bolt revenue figure after 2024.
- I found no sourced data on the market for AI courses or prompt packs specifically.
